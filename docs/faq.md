# FAQ

This FAQ answers practical questions about Sastra Innovations, Medha, MedhaOS, SastraPDF and this public documentation repository.

## What Is Medha?

Medha is Sastra's proprietary, multi-model reasoning and orchestration engine for context-aware enterprise workflows. Its public capability areas include retrieval-augmented generation, semantic retrieval, persistent context, semantic caching, task-aware model selection and governed workflow assistance. See the [Medha public documentation](https://github.com/sastra-innovations/medha-public-docs).

## What Is MedhaOS?

MedhaOS is the enterprise AI governance and control layer. Its public capability areas include identity, RBAC, tenant isolation, privacy controls, policy evaluation and enforcement boundaries, approvals, auditability, observability, approved model and provider paths and controlled execution. See the [MedhaOS public documentation](https://github.com/sastra-innovations/medha-os-public-docs).

## What Is SastraPDF?

SastraPDF is Sastra Innovations' document intelligence, collaborative editing and workflow platform for document-heavy enterprise processes. The current [SastraPDF public feature catalogue](https://github.com/sastra-innovations/sastrapdf-public-docs/blob/main/docs/features/feature-catalogue.md) documents 119 distinct document operations across ingestion, organization, editing, conversion, extraction, review, protection, accessibility, specialist document processing and workflow-related categories.

## How Are The Products Different?

Medha provides intelligence and reasoning. MedhaOS provides governance and operational control. SastraPDF provides document intelligence and document workflows.

## Can They Work Together?

Yes. The products are designed to support integrated enterprise workflows when reasoning, governance and document operations need to work together. Medha selects an appropriate reasoning path from the model and provider boundaries permitted by MedhaOS. SastraPDF supplies document content and executes permitted document actions. The products may also be evaluated individually according to their separate roles.

## What Is The Current Product Status?

The Sastra product stack has completed internal private-alpha testing. Current work focuses on technical hardening, documentation, beta restart preparation, defined-workflow validation and preparation for design-partner evaluation. The products remain pre-commercial and have not yet completed enterprise production validation.

## Is It Designed For Regulated Environments?

The stack is designed for governed enterprise workflows that may include privacy controls, RBAC, tenant isolation, approvals, auditability, observability and sensitive-data protection. Any regulated use should be evaluated against the organization's specific legal, security, compliance and operational requirements.

## Does SastraPDF Support More Than Basic PDF Editing?

Yes. SastraPDF is positioned as a document intelligence, collaborative editing and workflow platform, not merely a basic editing tool. The public feature catalogue documents 119 distinct document operations and separates those document operations from AI, collaboration, workflow and platform capabilities.

## Does Sastra Provide Implementation Services?

Yes. Sastra provides selective services across custom software, AI workflow automation, document and content intelligence, API and enterprise integration, application modernization, cloud, SaaS and database integration, dashboards, analytics, architecture and AI-governance consulting.

## Are Private Or Hybrid Deployments Supported?

Private and hybrid deployment patterns are part of Sastra's public architecture philosophy where required. Specific deployment fit depends on workflow scope, data sensitivity, integration requirements and enterprise review.

## Where Will Detailed Product Documentation Be Published?

This repository is the umbrella documentation hub. Detailed product-specific public documentation is now available in [medha-public-docs](https://github.com/sastra-innovations/medha-public-docs), [medha-os-public-docs](https://github.com/sastra-innovations/medha-os-public-docs) and [sastrapdf-public-docs](https://github.com/sastra-innovations/sastrapdf-public-docs).

## How Can Organizations Discuss A Design-Partner Pilot?

Organizations with defined document-heavy, information-heavy or governed workflow problems can contact Sastra through the [official contact page](https://sastra.io/contact). The [product status](product-status.md) page explains current evaluation expectations.
