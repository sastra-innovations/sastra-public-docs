# FAQ

This FAQ answers practical questions about Sastra Innovations, Medha, MedhaOS, SastraPDF and this public documentation repository.

## What Is Medha?

Medha is Sastra's proprietary multi-model AI reasoning and orchestration engine. Its public capability areas include retrieval-augmented generation, semantic retrieval, persistent context, model routing and governed workflow assistance.

## What Is MedhaOS?

MedhaOS is the enterprise AI governance and control layer. Its public capability areas include identity, RBAC, tenant isolation, privacy controls, policy enforcement, approvals, auditability, observability, model routing and controlled execution.

## What Is SastraPDF?

SastraPDF is a document intelligence and workflow platform supporting more than 70 document operations across extraction, review, transformation, processing and governed document actions.

## How Are The Products Different?

Medha provides intelligence and reasoning. MedhaOS provides governance and operational control. SastraPDF provides document intelligence and document workflows.

## Can They Work Together?

Yes. The products are designed to support integrated enterprise workflows when reasoning, governance and document operations need to work together. They may also be evaluated individually according to their separate roles.

## Is The Stack Production-Ready?

The stack has progressed through private-alpha development and validation, and current work focuses on technical hardening, documentation, beta preparation, defined workflow validation, design-partner engagement and enterprise pilot preparation. This repository should not be read as a broad availability claim for every enterprise environment.

## Is It Designed For Regulated Environments?

The stack is designed for governed enterprise workflows that may include privacy controls, RBAC, tenant isolation, approvals, auditability, observability and sensitive-data protection. Any regulated use should be evaluated against the organization's specific legal, security, compliance and operational requirements.

## Does SastraPDF Support More Than Basic PDF Editing?

Yes. SastraPDF is positioned as a document intelligence and workflow platform, not merely a basic editing tool. Public positioning describes support for more than 70 document operations across extraction, review, transformation, processing and governed document actions.

## Does Sastra Provide Implementation Services?

Yes. Sastra provides selective services across custom software, AI workflow automation, document and content intelligence, API and enterprise integration, application modernization, cloud, SaaS and database integration, dashboards, analytics, architecture and AI-governance consulting.

## Are Private Or Hybrid Deployments Supported?

Private and hybrid deployment patterns are part of Sastra's public architecture philosophy where required. Specific deployment fit depends on workflow scope, data sensitivity, integration requirements and enterprise review.

## Where Will Detailed Product Documentation Be Published?

This repository is the umbrella documentation hub. More detailed product-specific documentation is planned for `medha-public-docs`, `medha-os-public-docs` and `sastrapdf-public-docs`. Those repositories are not linked because they have not yet been created.

## How Can Organizations Discuss A Design-Partner Pilot?

Organizations with defined document-heavy, information-heavy or governed workflow problems can contact Sastra through the [official contact page](https://sastra.io/contact). The [product status](product-status.md) page explains current evaluation expectations.
