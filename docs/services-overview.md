# Services Overview

Sastra is product-led and service-capable. The company provides selective technology services that can support discovery, integration, workflow definition and adoption of governed AI and document intelligence systems without positioning Sastra as only a general outsourcing provider.

## Service Areas

| Service area | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Custom software and internal platforms | Design and delivery of internal business systems, workflow platforms and operational tools. |
| AI workflow automation | AI-assisted workflow definition, automation planning and governed execution patterns. |
| Document and content intelligence | Extraction, review, transformation and workflow design for document-heavy operations. |
| API and enterprise integration | Integration across SaaS platforms, internal systems, data sources and workflow destinations. |
| Application modernization | Modernization of legacy application flows, interfaces, integrations and operational processes. |
| Cloud, SaaS and database integration | Practical integration across cloud services, SaaS applications, databases and internal environments. |
| Dashboards and analytics | Operational dashboards, reporting layers and decision-support views. |
| Architecture and AI-governance consulting | Architecture review, workflow governance design, policy-aware AI patterns and implementation planning. |

## How Services Support Product Adoption

Sastra services can support organizations in four practical ways:

1. Discovery: define document-heavy, information-heavy or workflow-driven problems suitable for governed AI evaluation.
2. Workflow definition: map roles, permissions, policy checkpoints, approvals, source systems and expected outcomes.
3. Integration: connect product capabilities with enterprise systems, APIs, data sources and operational tools.
4. Validation: support design-partner or pilot planning with clear scope, governance expectations and review criteria.

## Product-Led Boundaries

Sastra services are intended to complement the Medha, MedhaOS and SastraPDF product stack. A services engagement may involve custom software, integration or workflow design, but the strategic focus remains governed enterprise AI, document intelligence and workflow automation.
