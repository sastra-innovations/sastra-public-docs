## 🚀 About Us
Sastra Innovations is an engineering-first company building the next generation of **AI-native enterprise platforms**. We combine deep platform engineering with pragmatic AI (RAG, Copilots, Semantic Search) to deliver secure, observable systems for regulated industries.

**Core Focus:**
- **AI-Native Operating Layers:** Integrating LLMs into core business workflows.
- **Digital Trust:** Auditability, provenance, and compliance-first architecture.
- **SaaS Engineering:** Scalable, multi-tenant architectures on modern stacks.

## 📦 Product Suite
We operate a suite of proprietary platforms and accelerators:

### 🧠 [Medha OS](https://medhaos.com)
A white-label **AI Operating Layer** that turns any legacy software into an intelligent system. Features include context-aware Q&A, semantic search, and autonomous workflow agents.

### 📄 [sastraPDF](https://sastrapdf.com)
An enterprise-grade **PDF Platform** combining an Acrobat-class web editor with secure SDKs. Designed for legal and financial teams who cannot send sensitive documents to public cloud APIs.

### 💼 [Jamaa.io](https://jamaa.io)
An **AI-Native ERP** for Indian businesses. Automates accounting, compliance, and resource planning with a conversational interface.

### 🔐 [Baatline](https://baatline.com)
Secure, verified business communication platform featuring **End-to-End Encryption (E2EE)** and identity-verified business accounts to eliminate impersonation fraud.
